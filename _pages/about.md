I am an MPhil student in **Smart Manufacturing** at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)](https://hkust-gz.edu.cn/), supervised by Prof. Pengcheng Hu. Before that, I received my B.Eng. in Automation from Shenyang University of Technology in 2026.

My research focuses on **metal additive manufacturing** — laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) process–property relationships, and **hybrid additive–subtractive manufacturing**, where LPBF parts are finished by multi-axis machining. I am also interested in process simulation and data-driven optimization of process parameters.

📧 Email: cpeng171@connect.hkust-gz.edu.cn
